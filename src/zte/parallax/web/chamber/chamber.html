Parallax Chamber

How to read this page. Three encoders were trained separately, one per ZuCo task, so the task-vs-stimulus confound is removed structurally rather than statistically. Every number here is a closed-set retrieval readout on the held-out subject; free generation is not measured on this page. Off-diagonal transfer cells face a never-seen subject reading never-seen sentences — a null there is a finding, reported plainly, not a failure of the page.

Capacity dials

Certified menu size K per encoder on a log-2 dial, from the exact-length-pool menu audit; larger K is better and chance is 1/K. When no K is certified the dial still shows the measured K=2 accuracy as a labelled needle against the chance (0.5) and target (0.8) ticks — a number, never a void. prototype = one reference per sentence; enrolled = best of the enrolled readings — when the audit carries both, the solid bar is the prototype and the bright needle is the enrolled reading.

CKA triad

thicker edge = closer geometry · CKA 1 = identical
__CHAMBER_MISS_TRIAD__

Edge thickness is linear CKA between two models' embeddings of the same sentences — thicker means closer geometry, on a 0 to 1 scale where 1 is identical. High CKA between encoders that never shared a stimulus is convergent geometry — the parallax reading of a task-invariant code. A dashed edge means that pair was not measured.
