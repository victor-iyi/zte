The greedy generation, token by token. The ribbon shows the word-synchronous evidence weights when the checkpoint carries that head — and the pointer walk is a fixed monotonic schedule, a function of step and word counts and never of content, so the diagonal band is the schedule, not a discovered alignment. Each spoke of the glyph is one prefix slot: its length is the mean absolute shift in the generated tokens' log-probabilities when that slot is zeroed and the same tokens are re-scored (nothing is regenerated). The right-hand box is the null control — the bridge's learned unconditional prefix, the LM with no brain attached. If the two boxes read alike, the brain contributed little; that is a finding, reported plainly.
